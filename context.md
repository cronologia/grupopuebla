# Project context

Pair with `AGENTS.md`; family method in `cronologia/core` (load sourcing-rules).

## The subject

**The Grupo de Puebla**: the forum of progressive Ibero-American INDIVIDUALS
founded 12–14 July 2019 in Puebla, Mexico (Declaración de Puebla, 14 July),
driven by Marco Enríquez-Ominami with Alberto Fernández, Ernesto Samper and
Aloizio Mercadante. Ten encuentros through Tegucigalpa (June 2024); CLAJUD
(the anti-'lawfare' council under Baltasar Garzón, launched January 2020);
the Manifiesto Progresista (2021). Signature causes: Lula's freedom, the
Bolivia 2019 'golpe' statement, Assange, the anti-Milei exchanges.

## The reason this project exists

The group is constantly conflated with the **Foro de São Paulo** (the fsp
project). They are distinct: 1990 vs 2019, parties vs individuals, and both
met separately, simultaneously, in Tegucigalpa in June 2024 — the cleanest
proof against the 'renaming' myth, which this project attributes to its
opinion-journalism sources and rebuts.
