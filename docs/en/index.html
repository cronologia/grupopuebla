Chronology of the Grupo de Puebla — the Latin American progressive forum of individuals (2019–present)

An open, source-referenced chronology of the Grupo de Puebla, the forum of Latin American (and southern-European) progressive leaders founded in Puebla, Mexico, in July 2019: its encuentros, declarations, CLAJUD, and its constantly conflated — but institutionally separate — relationship to the Foro de São Paulo.

About

Work-in-progress dataset on a young, politically contested organization. Official grupodepuebla.org pages are volatile (the site has already been reorganized) and are archived where possible; the 'renamed Foro de São Paulo' narrative comes from opinion journalism and is presented as an attributed claim, rebutted with the documented coexistence of both organizations; membership lists fluctuate and are dated. Corrections against primary sources are welcome.

What it is
A forum of progressive INDIVIDUALS — presidents, ex-presidents, ministers, legislators, academics — from Ibero-America, founded in Puebla, Mexico, in July 2019; no formal treaty headquarters, coordinated by an executive coordinator with support foundations.[4] [3]
Founded
12–14 July 2019, Puebla, Mexico, at the 'Encuentro Latinoamericano ProgresivaMente'; the Declaración de Puebla (14 July) constituted the 'Grupo Progresista Latinoamericano', quickly known as Grupo de Puebla.[1] [2] [3]
Coordination
Marco Enríquez-Ominami (Chile) is executive coordinator and the group's public face, with co-coordinators including Ernesto Samper; operational support from Fundación Progresa (Chile) and Corporación Escenarios (Colombia).[4] [3]
Key distinction
NOT the Foro de São Paulo renamed: the FSP (1990, São Paulo) is a forum of PARTIES; the Grupo de Puebla (2019) is a forum of individuals — both exist today and met separately, simultaneously, in Tegucigalpa in June 2024.[34] [32]
Signature causes
The anti-'lawfare' agenda (CLAJUD, launched January 2020 under Baltasar Garzón), Lula's freedom (2019), the Bolivia 2019 'golpe' statement, Assange's release, and successive declarations against the regional right.[12] [13] [10] [21]
Scale
Founded by ~30 leaders from about 12 countries; the current official founders list claims around 64 members from 18+ countries — the list fluctuates and is dated as retrieved. ?[4] [3]

Disambiguation & nuance

The group's name invites systematic conflations — these cards are the project's core service.

Grupo de Puebla ≠ Foro de São Paulo

FSP: founded 1990 in São Paulo, by PARTIES (including the Cuban CP). Grupo de Puebla: founded 2019 in Puebla, by INDIVIDUALS. Both are active today and met separately, simultaneously, in Tegucigalpa in June 2024 — the cleanest single proof against the 'renaming' myth, which comes from opinion journalism (Diario Las Américas, ADN América, Infobae opinion) and is presented here as an attributed claim. Overlap of individuals (Lula's orbit) explains, but does not justify, the conflation.[34] [48] [49]

≠ the Puebla Conference of 1979

CELAM's III General Conference (Puebla, January–February 1979, opened by John Paul II) shares only the city's name — zero relation; that event is documented in the sibling celam project.[3]

≠ the Progressive International

A distinct global organization (DiEM25/Sanders Institute, launched 2020) with movement-based membership; the Grupo de Puebla is an Ibero-American forum of individuals. They co-sign statements — which requires them to be different things.[46] [45]

≠ the 'Democracia Siempre' summit

The July 2025 Santiago summit was an initiative of sitting presidents that the group merely endorsed.[44]

The founding name

Constituted as 'Grupo Progresista Latinoamericano' at an event called 'ProgresivaMente'; the media consolidated 'Grupo de Puebla'.[2] [3]
